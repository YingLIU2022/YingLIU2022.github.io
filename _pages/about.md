### Hi!

My name is Ying Liu. I am currently a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate in the [School of Economics](https://economics.smu.edu.sg/), [Singapore Management University (SMU)](https://www.smu.edu.sg/).

My research interests are in applied microeconomics, with a focus on the economics of the health, and education. My current work examines how public policies and social environments shape outcomes for children, with particular attention to vulnerable populations in developing countries.
